Deterministic paper-trading engine for the India platform.

Derived from the internal Nirnay crates/nirnay-engine/src/{engine,state,market}.rs at source snapshot cc3a34c317a86a9984401efe90fa3f43d67a1787.

Responsibilities stay separated: trading-core owns contracts, [risk_engine] owns pre-trade risk, [cost_model] owns charge schedules and [account_journal] owns durable event storage. This crate owns order lifecycle, paper matching and the account projection.

Matching consumes the displayed top of book. It is a deterministic fill model, not a realistic historical execution simulator.
