Segmented, append-only account-event journal with exclusive writer ownership and a BLAKE3 hash chain.

Derived from the internal Nirnay crates/nirnay-journal/src/lib.rs at source snapshot cc3a34c317a86a9984401efe90fa3f43d67a1787.

This is the account/execution journal. The repository’s existing src/journal.rs remains the raw market-data frame journal; the two serve separate purposes and remain separate.

The on-disk frame format (NRJ1, 84-byte header, BLAKE3 chain over the sequence, previous hash and body) is preserved. Bodies are validated [trading_core::StoredBody] records with deterministic event identity.
