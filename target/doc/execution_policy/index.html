Central execution-mode policy (task P0-06, approval-authority revision).

Live authority is cryptographically enforced through independent attestations, not an issuer-signed list of names:

  • The issuer signs the permit body (commit, account, broker, strategy, instruments, limits, expiry) with the release key.
  • Every approver separately signs the issuer’s permit digest with their own Ed25519 key. Attestations bind to the issuer, the permit digest, and the expiry.
  • Verification requires one distinct valid attestation per counted approval, from a registered approver key. Duplicate identities and duplicate keys are rejected; revocation is checked on every call.
  • Registry configurations with a threshold above the registered approver count are rejected as malformed.

Construction security: a carried envelope never grants authority. Every authorization re-verifies the issuer signature and every attestation. The runtime clock, commit identity and projected exposure come from the trusted runtime and risk authorities, never from the strategy.

Enforcement boundary and its limits:

  • Mode: only an explicitly Live-configured policy can reach permit verification. Paper and Simulated policies deny Live authorization even when they carry a valid permit; a permit never changes the mode.
  • Scope: a permit authorizes only its account, broker, strategy, instrument allowlist, and financial limits. The permit schema has no venue/action/side fields, so those dimensions are NOT enforced — permissive assumptions about them are forbidden.
  • Time: a permit is expired at its expiry instant. Unrepresentable timestamps fail closed; they are never replaced with a fallback.
  • Limits: a request exactly at a limit is allowed; above denies.
  • Revocation: the registry supplied by the authority-owning application carries revocations. If that snapshot is stale or unavailable, the deployment — not this crate — owns re-fetching it; this crate only evaluates the registry it is given. Production use requires a fresh snapshot (a separate risk authority decision, not yet built).
  • Authorization is not reservation: this crate evaluates policy on the state it is given. Atomic exposure reservation, idempotency, and reconciliation belong to the independent risk authority, which does not exist yet. Do not describe this crate as an independent RMS.

Trust registry: issuer keys, approver keys, minimum approvals, and revocation list. Loaded from protected configuration; never constructed from strategy input.
TrustedKey
A registered public key. Fields are private; construction is the authority’s job, so untrusted input cannot pose as a trusted key. Every key is grammar-checked, hex-decoded, canonicalized, and Ed25519 validated at construction: invalid key material fails here, never at verification time.
TrustedRuntime
Runtime facts supplied by trusted authorities, not by the strategy. Fields are private so untrusted bytes cannot pose as trusted state. Construction is the authority-owning application’s responsibility.
