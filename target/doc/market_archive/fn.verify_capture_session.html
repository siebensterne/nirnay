Verify a capture session in one bounded pass over its journal.

The journal is streamed through the shared JournalReader while its bytes are hashed, so record validation, frame counting and whole-file hashing all observe the same stream. No frame collection or whole-archive buffering is performed. A terminal capture error is rejected before the journal opens.
