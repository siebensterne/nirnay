risk_authority

Module authority 

Source
Expand description

Stateful risk authority with one atomic authorization operation.

The authority owns every fact that evaluate_risk consumes: account-global exposure, positions, pending orders, rate state, daily P&L, the logical clock, snapshot availability, kill switch and reconciliation state. A caller supplies only an [OrderIntent] and raw observations from registered owner adapters; it cannot assert a Healthy flag, an exposure figure, an account snapshot, or forge another owner’s facts.

Evaluation and reservation happen under a single mutex lock, so two concurrent callers can never both evaluate against the same stale state. On full acceptance the reservation is applied before the lock is released; on any rejection nothing is reserved.
