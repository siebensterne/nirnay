Expand description

M2.4 stateful risk authority with atomic exposure reservation.

Sits in the engines layer and depends only on lower-layer crates. It owns account-global state, validates typed versioned observations from six owners and derives health inside one mutex. A caller supplies neither a Healthy flag nor an exposure figure. It produces reservations (permits) only; it has no OMS, broker, order submission or live-routing capability.
