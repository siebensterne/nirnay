Module observation 

Source
Expand description

Typed, versioned observations owned by the risk authority.

Callers submit raw facts from six independent owners. Every observation carries a source, schema version, observed and received logical times and a revision. The authority alone checks freshness and derives health; no caller-supplied verdict or boolean crosses this boundary.
