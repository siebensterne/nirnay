Fail-closed pre-trade risk evaluation.

Derived from the internal Nirnay crates/nirnay-core/src/risk.rs at source snapshot cc3a34c317a86a9984401efe90fa3f43d67a1787.

The policy is evaluated against the canonical India instrument model: listing activity comes from india-domain, quantities are non-negative lots, and every cash limit is exact paise. A policy that fails validation is never evaluated; an evaluation returns every check so a rejection can be explained rather than inferred.

Structs§

GreekLimits
Typed placeholder limits for synthetic per-instrument Greeks. Sensitivities use the same 10^6 fixed-point scaling as [Greeks].
RiskContext
Point-in-time account and market state used for one evaluation.
RiskPolicy
Effective risk policy. All limits are mandatory; validation rejects an empty allowlist, a zero limit or an impossible collar. The sector, stress, liquidity, Greeks and borrow controls are opt-in: None means the control has no configured cap or reference data, and the corresponding check is inert. Once configured, each control fails closed on missing evidence.
StressScenario
Deterministic, uncalibrated stress scenario. A negative price_shift_ppm is a downward parallel shift. Every term is an exact integer ratio in ppm; no floating point is used in projected-loss arithmetic.
ValidatedRiskPolicy
A risk policy validated for evaluation. There is exactly one source of a ValidatedRiskPolicy: ValidatedRiskPolicy::from_policy, which runs RiskPolicy::validate first. An unvalidated policy can never reach evaluate_risk; deserialization is equally unable to skip the check because construction, not serde, is the only entry point.
