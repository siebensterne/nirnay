# RUSTSEC-2026-0592 narrow remediation — 2026-11-22

**Status:** narrow dependency remediation applied; no waiver, no blanket suppression.

## Advisory

- ID: `RUSTSEC-2026-0592` — "Stack overflow in `Url::origin()` on nested `blob:` URLs".
- Affected: `url` 2.5.8 and earlier. Patched: `url` >= 2.5.9.
- Mechanism: `Url::origin()` recursed on nested `blob:` URLs without a depth limit; a
  sufficiently nested input overflows the stack and aborts the process. `catch_unwind`
  does not catch a stack overflow.
- Owner reference: <https://rustsec.org/advisories/RUSTSEC-2026-0592>.

## Reachability check (before any change)

`cargo tree -i url --locked` on the reviewed base showed the crate is present and
reachable in the workspace build graph:

```text
url v2.5.8
├── reqwest v0.12.28
│   └── upstox-tools v0.4.0 (apps/upstox-tools)
└── tower-http v0.6.11
    └── reqwest v0.12.28 (*)
```

A source scan of `crates/**/*.rs` and `apps/**/*.rs` found no direct call to
`Url::origin()` or `url::quirks::origin()` in workspace code. The advisory is
therefore a reachable dependency exposure rather than a confirmed call-site
exploit in first-party code. No claim of exploitability is made.

## Change applied

- `Cargo.lock`: `url` 2.5.8 → 2.5.9 (the patched release named by the advisory).
- `Cargo.lock`: `form_urlencoded` 1.2.2 → 1.2.3, pulled in by the `url` 2.5.9 release.
- No manifest (`Cargo.toml`) version constraint was widened.
- No advisory was ignored, suppressed or added to an allow-list.
